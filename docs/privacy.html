Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Advinas is an independent community Discord bot operated by Sprylos and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Prineside or the creators of Infinitode 2.

Information used

Advinas processes information needed for its features, including Discord user, server, channel and role information; messages and attachments; member, presence and voice information; and linked Infinitode account or game data. This information is used for features such as moderation, commands, account linking, contests, game information and music playback.

Storage

Feature data such as linked Infinitode accounts and user-created tags may be stored in Advinas' database. Command and diagnostic logs, moderation records and contest submissions may also be retained in Discord channels and can include message content or submitted attachments. These records do not currently expire automatically and remain until manually removed.

Third-party services

Advinas uses Discord, Infinitode/Prineside services and music services to provide its features. Information needed for a feature may be sent to those services and is subject to their own privacy policies.

Your data

You can unlink your Infinitode account or remove tags you control through the bot. To request deletion of other information held by Advinas, contact Sprylos by private message on Discord through the community server. Requests may require verification that the data belongs to you.

Website

Advinas does not add analytics, tracking scripts or browser storage to this website. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process connection information under GitHub's own privacy practices. This policy may be updated as Advinas changes.
